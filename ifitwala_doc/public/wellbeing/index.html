Hierarchical Clarity

Ifitwala Ed models your real-world structure with precision — schools within schools, organizations within organizations.

Define who sees what: parent-school oversight or child-unit autonomy. Permissions cascade intelligently through the hierarchy.

With Ifitwala Ed, you don’t just assign roles — you define context. An educator or administrator at a parent organisation or school gains view and edit rights across all descendant schools or subsidiary organisations — giving you the clarity to monitor what matters most in your domain. Meanwhile, each child-unit retains its independence: local staff operate within their own scope, yet the higher-level team can step in when needed. This balance of oversight and autonomy ensures you stay informed without micromanaging — because structure matters, but so does trust.

Multi-Campus Cohesion

If you oversee several schools, campuses or departments, you’ll appreciate how Ifitwala Ed allows you to define calendars, terms, scheduling and assessments at any level — whether at the parent organisation or at the embedded child school. Each site can operate with its own timetable, academic year or session while shared programs, assessments or reports can be coordinated centrally. You get unified metrics, consistent processes and shared insight — yet each location retains the flexibility to address its unique needs. The result: cohesion across your network, without surrendering local agility.
