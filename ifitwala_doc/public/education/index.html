Why this matters

Schools reveal the hardest parts of systems work.

Education data is personal, operational, time-sensitive, and distributed across many roles. A weak workflow can become a reporting issue, a privacy issue, or a student support issue.

That makes education a strong base for broader ERP and governance work: it forces attention to permissions, adoption, data quality, reporting, and practical controls.
